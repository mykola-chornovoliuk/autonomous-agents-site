02 / The operating loop

From signal to verified completion.

The difficult part is not generating text. It is preserving intent, assigning the next action, tracking the outcome and safely handling exceptions across connected systems.

01 / SIGNAL

Detect & understand

Capture an authorized email, document, event or system change. Extract intent and supporting evidence.

02 / ACTION

Plan & execute

Determine owner, next step and deadline. Create a task or draft an action inside the permitted tools.

03 / CONTROL

Follow up & verify

Track status and exceptions, request approvals and confirm the result against an observable outcome.

Example / communications

Decision from a meeting

Meeting notes become a decision register with owners, due dates and follow-up drafts.

Measure: missed actions · review time · completion rate
Example / documents

Invoice discrepancy

Compare invoice lines with the purchase order, flag mismatches and prepare a resolution request.

Measure: detection precision · processing time
Example / operations

Unclosed commitment

Detect a promised deliverable without closure; ask the responsible person for status and log the response.

Measure: overdue commitments · false alerts
03 / Guardrails

Autonomy with boundaries.

Every agent operates within explicit scopes, access permissions and review rules. We do not treat generated text as verified execution.

Human approval

Payments, purchases, submissions and sensitive external messages require explicit authorization.

Evidence & traceability

Source links, action logs, uncertainty flags and completion checks wherever systems permit.

Least privilege

Only approved data and integrations; secrets stay server-side, never exposed in a website.

Recovery by design

Failures and ambiguous cases escalate instead of silently repeating risky actions.
